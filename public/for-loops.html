For Loops & Iterables

Iterate efficiently across sequences, lists, strings, and numerical ranges.

🔁 Stepping Through Collections with for

While a while loop runs based on a condition, a for loop is designed to iterate through a known collection of items (called an iterable), such as a list of items, characters in a string, or a numerical sequence.

conveyor_belt.py ● Sequence Iterator
robots = ["Atlas", "Orion", "Titan"]

for bot in robots:
  print(f"Calibrating unit: {bot}")

⚙️ The Magic of range() & Interactive Simulation

When you want to repeat a task a specific number of times without a pre-existing list, Python's built-in range(start, stop) function automatically generates a sequence of numbers on the fly!

Index 0: "Alpha"
Index 1: "Beta"
Index 2: "Gamma"
Index 3: "Delta"

for i in range(4):
